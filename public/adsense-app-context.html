HabTastic AI

HabTastic AI is a habit-tracking and productivity app with daily habit check-ins, streaks, progress reporting, AI coaching, savings goals, community features and gamified rewards.

Free plan experience

  • Up to 4 active habits.
  • Up to 5 Winston AI coaching messages per UTC day.
  • One active Money Saver savings goal.
  • Progress, streak and community features.
  • Advertising supports the Free plan on web/PWA and native mobile.

New accounts receive a 5-day Premium trial. Premium and active-trial users are ad-free and receive the expanded Premium feature set.

Authenticated app content

Signed-in users see their own habits, completion history, progress, streaks, coaching conversations and savings information. That personal data remains protected behind authentication and is not exposed on this public page. This page exists so search and advertising crawlers can understand the type of content and product experience surrounding ads without being given access to a real user account.

Advertising

Free users in the Android app are served ads through Google AdMob. Free users in the browser/PWA are eligible for Google AdSense ads. Premium subscribers and users in the active Premium trial do not receive ads.
